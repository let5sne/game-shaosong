Privacy & Licenses

The Register · A Paper Campaign

What this game does not do

The Register is a single-page browser game with no backend, no account and no analytics. It makes no network requests beyond loading its own static files. There are no cookies, no advertising SDKs, no telemetry and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do in a session is uploaded anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Saved campaigns, settings and preferences live only in your browser's localStorage (keys prefixed shaosong.).
  • URL parameters such as seed, lang and scenario are read locally and written back to the address bar. They are part of a shareable link, not a report to a server.
  • Clearing site data in your browser removes all of it. There is no copy on our side to delete.

If you play inside an embedded frame (for example on itch.io), browser storage policies may isolate or block local saves. Exporting a replay file is the recommended way to keep a campaign in that case.

版籍 · 行台北伐

本游戏不做什么

《版籍》是单页浏览器游戏，无后端、无账号、无统计埋点。除加载本站静态文件外不发起任何网络请求； 没有 cookie、广告 SDK、遥测或第三方追踪。玩家在本机的所有操作都不会上传。

留在本机的数据

  • 存档、设置与偏好只存于浏览器的 localStorage（键名以 shaosong. 开头）。
  • seed、lang、scenario 等链接参数只在本机读取并写回地址栏， 属于可分享链接的一部分，不会发往服务器。
  • 在浏览器中清除本站数据即可全部删除；我们没有留副本。

若在嵌入式框架内游玩（例如 itch.io），浏览器的存储策略可能隔离或阻止本地存档， 此时建议用复盘文件导出／导入来保留局面。

  • three 与 vite：MIT 许可证。
  • Noto Serif SC 字体子集：SIL OFL 1.1（public/fonts/OFL.txt）。
